## Reusable Prompts For Agents

### Article Brief Prompt

Write a content brief for Tuto. Target adult/professional learners. The product promise is: "Turn trusted PDFs, notes, or topics into guided courses with practice and review." Avoid claims about guaranteed learning outcomes. Include search intent, outline, product demo angle, objections, and CTA.

### Repurposing Prompt

Repurpose this approved article into: one LinkedIn post, one X thread, one 45-second video script, one newsletter intro, and one community answer. Keep claims conservative, concrete, and grounded in the product workflow.

### Weekly GTM Brief Prompt

Research AI learning tools, PDF-to-study workflows, and adult learner complaints from the last week. Summarize competitor moves, user pain language, content ideas, and one landing page copy test. Flag anything that needs human verification.

## Beta Interview Questions

- What material did you want to learn from first?
- Why that material?
- What would make a generated course trustworthy enough to use?
- Where did the first course creation flow feel unclear?
- What did Tuto do that a normal chatbot did not?
- What did you expect that Tuto did not do?
- Would you pay $20/month for this? Why or why not?
- What result would make you invite a friend or colleague?
- What privacy concern would block you from uploading material?
- Which words would you use to describe Tuto to someone else?
